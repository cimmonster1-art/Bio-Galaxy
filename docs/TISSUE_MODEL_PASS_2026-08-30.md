# Tissue model pass — 2026-08-30

The tissue layer was rebuilt to remove the repeated pink-cylinder look and make the scale read as skeletal muscle tissue rather than stylised candy.

## Changes

- Replaced mathematically perfect giant cylinders with irregular capped organic fibre geometry.
- Grouped fibres into four fascicles with perimysial sheaths and subtle endomysial collagen.
- Replaced saturated emissive pink materials with desaturated, rough, non-emissive tissue shading and restrained surface grain.
- Preserved authored transparency during cross-fades instead of fading every material toward full opacity.
- Replaced glowing red point sprites with instanced biconcave erythrocytes travelling through curved capillaries.
- Replaced the bright yellow neuron treatment with a muted motor axon and terminal arbor.
- Removed additive haze/sparkle treatment and replaced it with quiet interstitial particles and neutral biological lighting.
- Made contraction motion nearly imperceptible so the scene reads as tissue rather than an animated prop.
