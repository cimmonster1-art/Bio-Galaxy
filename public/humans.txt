/* BIO GALAXY */
Bio Galaxy is an open-source interactive 3D biological atlas built over public scientific databases.

/* TECHNOLOGY */
React, TypeScript, Three.js, WebGL, Vite, Express, glTF

/* DATA */
UniProt, Reactome, RCSB PDB, Ensembl, Human Protein Atlas, NCBI

/* PRINCIPLES */
Source-aware records, key-free public endpoints, explicit attribution, modular rendering, and complete Three.js resource cleanup.
